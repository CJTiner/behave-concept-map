# Anatomical model sources

## BodyParts3D 4.0 anatomical meshes

BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International.

- Source: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
- Current archive license: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original archive: partof_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip, with partof_element_parts.txt.

The model includes selected skin, bones, brain, heart, adrenal and eye structures. Meshes have been combined by display layer, quantized to 0.1 mm, compressed, recolored and repositioned for this project. The original OBJ headers also retain the historical CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan notice; this notice is preserved here: BodyParts3D, (c) The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan.

## BodyParts3D 4.3 nerve and spinal-cord meshes

## Interpretation

The source anatomy is a reference model, not a complete representation of every nerve or tissue. Reading-topic pins and concept-map links are approximate educational annotations, not verified neural pathways. No source-model geometry establishes that a topic appears in Chapters 1–3 of Behave. All book explanations remain student-supplied placeholders.
